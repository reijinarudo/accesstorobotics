No harm done. Nothing was charged.

Your donation was canceled before it completed, and no payment was made. People step back for all kinds of good reasons, and we would rather you give with full confidence or not at all.

If something gave you pause

Questions are welcome here.

If you stepped back because of a question, about where the money goes, about our tax status, about who we are, those are exactly the questions a careful donor should ask, and we answer all of them plainly. The Donors page covers our tax status disclosure and giving tiers, and an itemized breakdown of program costs is available to anyone who asks.

If online payment was the obstacle, donations by check or bank transfer are equally welcome, with instructions available through the contact page. And if the timing simply is not right, there are other ways to help: mentorship hours, in-kind support, or introducing us to a school that should know about the program.

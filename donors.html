Support the Initiative

Concrete dollars, concrete students.

The Initiative is funded entirely by private contributions and in-kind partnerships. Every dollar reaches a scholar in a form they can use: hardware, shipping, mentorship infrastructure, or programmatic operations.

A note on tax status

The Initiative is incorporated as a Georgia nonprofit corporation (July 2026), and our application for federal 501(c)(3) recognition is in preparation. Until the IRS grants that recognition, contributions are not tax-deductible. Deductibility is expected, though not guaranteed, to apply retroactively once granted; we will update this page and notify donors when it does.

Current campaign

Fund the Fleet.

Goal: $2,500 · five robots

The Initiative is building a fleet of five Reachy Mini robots for small-group, hands-on workshops, sessions where students do not watch a robot demonstration, they sit down at one, two students per robot, and program it themselves.

The arithmetic could not be simpler. Each robot costs about $500. Each robot funded adds two student seats to every workshop the Initiative will ever run, for the life of the hardware. Five robots outfit the full fleet.

Gifts of any size count toward the fleet, five friends at $100 fund a robot together. Mark your gift "fleet" in the PayPal note, or tell us directly, and that is exactly where it goes.

A word on timing, because honesty is part of the work: each robot takes three to four months to arrive after ordering, and every one needs configuration and testing before it meets a student. Hands-on workshops are coming, but they are a 2027 milestone, not a next-month promise. The meter above is the schedule. The sooner the fleet is funded, the sooner the first session seats a student.

01 / Where the money goes

Levels of support.

2026–2027 cycle

The tiers below fund the scholarship program: a Reachy Mini, shipping, and a year of mentorship sent directly to individual student awardees. This is separate from the Fund the Fleet campaign above, which equips group workshops. A fleet gift seats students at a workshop table; a scholarship gift puts a robot in one scholar's home.

Tier 01 — Component Sponsor

$100 to $499

Funds a portion of a single scholarship: shipping, a component upgrade, or a session of mentorship infrastructure. Recognition on the Donors page if desired.

Tier 02 — Scholarship Sponsor

$500 to $1,999

Substantial contribution toward a single scholar's full program year. Named recognition in the cohort year announcement.

Tier 03 — Cohort Partner

$2,000 and above

Underwrites a named scholarship slot for the cohort. Named acknowledgment in scholar communications and on the year's Showcase. Optional invitation to the year-end review.

For transparency: cohort-level support maps directly to scholar slots, and a Tier 03 contribution fully underwrites one scholar's hardware, shipping, and mentorship year. An itemized breakdown is available to any donor on request.

Scholarship gifts are held for the scholarship program, period. They are never redirected to workshops, the fleet, or general operations. To support the workshop fleet instead, give through the Fund the Fleet campaign above.

Prefer not to choose? That is welcome too. Gifts made without a designation go to the Initiative's general fund, and the board applies them where the need is greatest, whether that is a scholar, the fleet, or the operations that keep both running.

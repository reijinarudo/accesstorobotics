Thank you. This is exactly how it happens.

Programs like this one are not built by institutions. They are built by individual people who decide a student they will never meet deserves a real chance. Today, that was you.

What happens next

Your gift gets to work immediately.

PayPal will send you a payment confirmation for your records. Your contribution goes directly toward the program: scholar hardware, shipping, mentorship infrastructure, and the workshop fleet that puts robots into students' hands.

A reminder on tax status, because we would rather repeat it than obscure it: the Initiative's 501(c)(3) application is in progress, and until recognition is granted, contributions are not tax-deductible. If recognition is granted, deductibility is expected to apply retroactively to contributions made after our July 2026 incorporation, though this cannot be guaranteed. Prior donors will be notified either way.

If you would like your gift directed somewhere specific, the workshop fleet, a scholar's hardware, shipping, tell us and that is where it goes. And if you would like to be recognized on the Donors page, or prefer to remain anonymous, let us know that too.
